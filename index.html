LOGIC MINIMIZER & K-MAP

Type an expression or click the map. Quine–McCluskey finds every prime implicant, then a branch-and-bound exact cover picks a provably minimal one — fewest terms first, then fewest literals. Not a greedy guess.

Single letters are variables (so AB means A AND B). ' ! ~ negate, & * · are AND, + | are OR, ^ is XOR, and AND OR NOT XOR NAND NOR XNOR work as words. Constants 0 and 1 too.

Click a Karnaugh cell to cycle it 0 → 1 → X. X is a don't-care: the minimiser may use it or ignore it, whichever makes the result smaller. Cells are Gray-coded, so neighbours — including across an edge — differ in one variable.

Prime implicants

Every implicant that cannot be widened further. Essential ones cover a 1 no other implicant reaches, so they are in every minimal cover; chosen marks the rest of the cover picked here. Hover or tap one to light it up on the map.
